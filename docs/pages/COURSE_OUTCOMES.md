# COURSE_OUTCOMES

**URL:** `/my/<org-slug>/courses/<course_id>/outcomes`  
**URL map:** [URLS.md](../URLS.md)

## Audience

Staff who can view the course (people who can manage it, and observers). Parents and learners do not open this editor.

## Purpose

Define what students should be able to do by the end of this course, and optional criteria under each outcome.


## Behavior

- Loads the course’s outcomes in sort order. Archived outcomes sit in a separate list.
- People who can manage the course add, rename, reorder, and archive outcomes, and add, rename, reorder, and remove criteria.
- Observers see the same lists read-only.
- An outcome with no criteria is rated as a whole later. An outcome with criteria is rated per criterion later. This page does not record ratings.
- Empty list explains that the next step is to add an outcome.
- Archiving hides an outcome from the active list. Restore brings it back.

## Data shown

- Course title
- Each active outcome: statement, ordered criteria
- Archived outcomes: statement

## Contents

- Outcome cards
- Add outcome
- Archived list
- Link back to the course

## Primary actions

- Add, rename, reorder, or archive an outcome
- Add, rename, reorder, or remove a criterion
- Restore an archived outcome

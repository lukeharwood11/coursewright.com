# HOME

**URL:** `/`  
**URL map:** [URLS.md](../URLS.md)

## Audience

Families managing their own courses and materials, plus directors and teachers at co-ops and small schools. Tech-averse people opening the student experience remain the design constraint.

## Purpose

P0 sales landing. Explain the job — this week’s materials into families’ hands, on paper if needed — and drive free signup or plan evaluation.


## Behavior

- Public, unauthenticated marketing page.
- Primary hero CTA goes to signup. The hero’s secondary CTA scrolls to `#how-it-works`. Opening `/#this-week` scrolls to the feature section. The closing secondary CTA goes to pricing.
- No org or course data loaded. The parent preview is a static illustration.

## Data shown

- Hero headline and subhead (static), matching the pre-ladder homepage
- Static parent preview with **Print this week**
- Patchwork comparison (static). Generic words only — do not name third-party brands.
- Four benefits and a three-step explanation of how the product works
- Links/CTAs only — no live product entities

## Contents

- Audience line: For families, co-ops, and small schools
- Headline: Courses, done wright.
- Subhead: plan courses, organize materials, and keep each week clear on screen or on paper — simple enough for a family and flexible enough for a small school
- Primary CTA: **Start free** → [SIGNUP](./SIGNUP.md)
- Secondary CTA: **See how it works** → `#how-it-works`
- `#this-week` scrolls to the benefit section
- Problem comparison: **The patchwork** versus **With Course Wright**
- Four benefits: Print the whole week in one tap; Keep every family on the same page; Everything for the course, together; Built for small, flexible programs
- How it works: Build the course; Use or share the week; Teach, reuse, and grow
- Audience context: families managing their own learning, plus directors, teachers, and volunteers running co-ops and small schools
- Closing: **Start with one course and two students — for free.** Primary **Start free** → [SIGNUP](./SIGNUP.md). Secondary **See pricing** → [PRICING](./PRICING.md)
